
App features:

Users can choose to sign in (no full sign in feature planned, just for the sake of accessing a worker or company account, so perhaps a password/username added to those, but not necssarily full security)



As a company:
    update company details
    add/update new job type
    add new shift for a job type
    cancel shift for a job type
    agree to worker for shift
    give performance reviews for workers for a job

As a worker:
    update worker details
    update/add to schedule
    view possible jobs for schedule/skills
    agree to sign up for shift

The app itself will:
    compile average worker score (to serve to company when choosing to accept employee)
    calculate which jobs a worker is available for
    calculate which workers a job has access to
